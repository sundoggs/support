# Security

Please report security problems **privately**, never as a public issue:

- GitHub: [Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/sundoggs/support/security/advisories/new) in this repository, or
- email **security@sundo.gs**.

Include the app and version, what an attacker could do, and steps to reproduce. Don't include real visitors' data.
We'll acknowledge your report and keep you updated while we fix it. The store's full policy and contact details are at
https://sundo.gs/.well-known/security.txt.
