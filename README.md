# Sundogs support

Public place to report problems and request features for [Sundogs](https://sundo.gs) site apps: mini apps that your
AI coding agent installs into your own Cloudflare® account.

## Report something

[**Open an issue**](https://github.com/sundoggs/support/issues/new/choose) and pick a form: bug report, feature
request, new app request or question. Many apps are in **beta**: they work, but things may still break. Reports here
are how we find and fix those fast.

A good bug report has:
- the app and its version, and the site layer (core) version (admin → Settings → Apps);
- where your site runs (Pages or Workers);
- what happened and what you expected;
- the self-test output (admin → Site Health, or your agent's `site_self_test` tool).

Your AI agent can file it for you: ask it to "file a Sundogs bug report with the self-test output".

**Never post** API keys, tokens, passwords, licence files or your visitors' data. This page is public.

## Not for here

- **Security problems:** report privately under [Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/sundoggs/support/security/advisories/new), or email security@sundo.gs.
- **Orders, refunds, account or billing:** email support@sundo.gs or use [sundo.gs/contact](https://sundo.gs/contact).
